## Goal
Create a high-end professional portfolio that presents Munjed as a full-stack developer, SaaS builder, digital systems architect, and public-sector digital transformation practitioner.

## Visual Direction
- Institutional, editorial, engineering-oriented.
- Neutral warm background, charcoal typography, restrained dark-red accent.
- No neon purple/blue gradients or glowing AI visuals.
- No fake skill percentages or generic startup copy.
- Prefer architecture, maturity labels, verified screenshots, and operational evidence.

## Accuracy Rules
- Distinguish operational systems, advanced products, MVPs, prototypes, foundations, and concepts.
- Never inflate implementation status.
- Verify repository claims before publishing detailed case studies.
- Private repositories may be described at a safe high level; never expose private source or confidential data.
- Public-sector work must not publish credentials, citizen records, internal network details, sensitive operational data, or security-relevant deployment information.
